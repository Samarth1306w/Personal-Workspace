Sales Channel Partner Agreement

Between Bridge Builders AI LLC and Samarth Nimangre. Schedule A (Sales Channel Partner) is attached and forms part of this Agreement.

1. Parties

This Agreement is between Bridge Builders AI LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Bridge Builders"), and Samarth Nimangre, an individual ("Contractor"). Effective date: the later of the dates on which Bridge Builders and Contractor sign below.

2. Independent contractor relationship

2.1 Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee, partner, joint venturer, member, or agent of Bridge Builders.

2.2 Contractor decides when, where, how, and how much to prospect. Bridge Builders sets the products, the published prices, the qualification criteria, the handoff standard, and the approved materials. Bridge Builders does not set hours, quotas, territories, or methods.

2.3 Contractor supplies their own equipment, software, workspace, connectivity, and prospecting channels. Bridge Builders supplies no leads.

2.4 Contractor may perform work for others, subject to Sections 7, 8, and 11.

2.4a No minimum activity. Contractor has no quota and no minimum activity, volume or result obligation. Producing no deals in any period is not a breach, not a ground for termination for cause, and not a factor in the attribution or payment of deals Contractor does produce. A handoff does not oblige Bridge Builders to sign the client: Bridge Builders may decline a prospect that does not meet the criteria in Schedule A, or that it reasonably determines it cannot serve, and will tell Contractor in writing when it declines one and why.

2.5 Contractor is responsible for all taxes on amounts paid under this Agreement. Bridge Builders will not withhold taxes except where United States law requires it, and will issue information returns as Section 15 describes. Before the first payment Contractor delivers the tax form in Section 15 that matches its status.

2.6 Contractor receives no salary, base pay, draw, benefits, insurance, paid leave, equity, profit share, or membership interest in Bridge Builders.

2.7 Where Contractor is an entity rather than an individual, the individuals who will perform the Services are listed in Schedule A ("Named Personnel"). Only Named Personnel may perform the Services. Contractor will not subcontract, assign, or delegate any part of the Services, and will not add or replace Named Personnel, without Bridge Builders' prior written consent.

2.8 Allocation of obligations for entity Contractors. Where Contractor is an entity, it is responsible for its Named Personnel complying with Sections 7 through 12, and is liable to Bridge Builders for their acts and omissions in performing the Services as if they were Contractor's own.

Named Personnel sign the joinder in Section 17, by which each becomes a party to this Agreement for those Sections only, and are personally bound by Sections 7 (confidentiality), 9 (prospect data handling) and 12 (lawful lead sourcing) only, which carry Section 14 (dispute resolution) with them for disputes about those obligations (7.5, 9.7, 12.5, 14.4b). Section 12 is included in that personal undertaking because a person who dials a number or sends an email is the person whose conduct creates the exposure, and no entity structure changes that.

The commercial obligations in Sections 8 (intellectual property), 10 (no authority to bind) and 11 (non-solicitation) are Contractor's as an entity, and are not undertaken personally by individuals who are not parties to this Agreement.

3. Services and deliverables

3.1 Contractor will source, qualify, and present prospective clients to Bridge Builders and hand closed clients to Bridge Builders as described in Schedule A (the "Services").

3.2 A completed handoff packet, in the form Bridge Builders specifies, is the deliverable for each client. The qualification criteria and the handoff standard are stated in Schedule A and in the Bridge Builders documents it names. Bridge Builders will give Contractor the current version of each document Schedule A names before Contractor begins the Services and on each change, and Contractor is bound only by a version it has been given.

3.3 Contractor will perform the Services honestly and in a professional manner consistent with those documents.

4. Compensation

4.1 Bridge Builders will pay Contractor the implementation commission and the monthly residual set out in the compensation table in Schedule A. No other compensation is owed.

4.2 Payment on collection. No amount is earned or payable until Bridge Builders has collected the corresponding client payment and it has cleared. A signed client contract earns nothing by itself. If a client never pays, nothing is owed on that deal.

Collection failures that are Bridge Builders' own. If a client's implementation fee or monthly fee, once due, is not collected, or is collected in a reduced amount, because Bridge Builders waived, discounted or credited it, failed to invoice it, or failed to maintain the means of collection, the commission or residual on that fee is computed and payable as if the published price had been collected when the fee fell due, and the deal counts toward that month under A2. If Bridge Builders ends a client's contract for a reason other than the client's non-payment or breach, the residual for the month in which it ends is payable as if collected, and residuals for that client then end, subject to the return rule in A2. A change to the published prices under 16.2 is not a discount.

4.3 Payment timing. Bridge Builders will prepare a monthly statement of amounts earned and pay the statement total by the 10th day of the following month. The statement shows each client attributed to Contractor separately and states, for each, whether that client's fee for the month was collected, so Contractor is not left to infer it. Contractor may raise a statement dispute in writing within 15 days of receiving it, and Bridge Builders will respond in writing within 10 days; amounts not disputed are paid on schedule, and any disputed amount Bridge Builders accepts as owed in its response is paid within 10 days of that response. A statement not disputed within that window is final, except for an arithmetic error or an omitted client that Bridge Builders' own records show. On written request, not more than twice in any 12 months, Bridge Builders will give Contractor the collection records for the clients attributed to Contractor that support a statement.

4.4 Clawback. If Bridge Builders refunds all or part of a client's implementation fee within 90 days after collecting it, Contractor will repay the same proportion of the implementation commission received on that deal, and Bridge Builders may offset it against future amounts owed; any balance not offset within 90 days is repaid within 30 days of written request. No repayment is owed where the refund is made for a reason other than the client's own cancellation, failure to cooperate or breach (for example, because Bridge Builders or its implementer failed to perform), or where the refund is made more than 90 days after collection. A refund does not change the tier applied to Contractor's other deals in that month. A client cancellation without a refund of the implementation fee triggers no repayment. Residuals already paid are never repaid.

4.5 Expenses. Contractor bears their own expenses, including every prospecting cost. Bridge Builders reimburses only expenses it approved in writing before they were incurred.

5. Term and termination

5.1 This Agreement begins on the effective date and continues until terminated.

5.2 Either party may terminate for convenience on 14 days' written notice.

5.3 Either party may terminate immediately on written notice for material breach of Sections 7, 8, 9, 10, or 12, or for any other material breach that is not capable of cure. For any other material breach, the terminating party first gives written notice describing the breach and 10 days to cure it, and may terminate on further written notice if the breach is not cured.

5.4 On termination, Contractor will stop the Services, stop presenting Bridge Builders products, return or destroy Bridge Builders' Confidential Information (other than copies of its own records of this engagement that it must keep by law, and the records 15.3 requires it to keep, none of which may contain client customer data and all of which stay subject to Section 7), and give the written certification in 9.5.

5.5 Pay after this Agreement ends. Amounts earned before the end date are paid in the ordinary course on the statement timetable in 4.3, including residuals on monthly fees collected before the end date. Residuals end on the end date in every case: no residual is earned on a fee collected after it. After the end date:

(a) Ended by Bridge Builders, under 5.2 or 5.3: nothing further is earned. A deal whose implementation fee has not been collected by the end date belongs to Bridge Builders when it closes, and no commission is owed on it.

(b) Ended by Contractor on notice under 5.2, or under 5.3 for Bridge Builders' breach: Contractor may finish the deals it had handed off under A1 or registered under A3 before the end date ("open deals"). An open deal whose implementation fee Bridge Builders collects within 60 days after the end date earns the implementation commission on the terms in Section 4 and Schedule A. The client then belongs to Bridge Builders, and no residual is earned on it. Commission under this paragraph ends, on written notice, if Contractor breaches Section 7, 9, 10, 11 or 12 after the end date.

(c) Wind-down. If Bridge Builders stops providing its products to its clients generally, whether because it closes, winds down, or can no longer operate, this Agreement ends on the date the products stop. The rule in 4.2 on collection failures that are Bridge Builders' own does not apply to any fee falling due after that date, and nothing is earned on money Bridge Builders does not collect.

Repayment under 4.4 continues to apply after the end date to commission already paid; any amount not offset is repaid within 30 days of written request. Residuals already paid are never repaid.

9. Data protection and prospect data handling

9.1 Contractor never holds access to a client's systems, a client's accounts, or any Bridge Builders production system, other than the handoff systems Bridge Builders designates under 9.3 and, in those, only the access Bridge Builders grants. 9.2 Contractor will never accept, request, store, or use a client's or prospect's account password, and will never collect payment. Clients pay Bridge Builders directly.

9.5 On termination, Contractor will certify in writing that no handoff packet, discovery note, or prospect or client data received from Bridge Builders or from a client, or gathered from a prospect in discovery, under this Agreement remains in their possession, other than the contact records that 9.3 leaves with Contractor.

9.6 Contractor will report any suspected security incident involving Bridge Builders or client data to Bridge Builders within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.

9.7 Disputes about this Section. A dispute about an obligation under this Section, including one with a Named Person bound by it under Section 17, is resolved under Section 14, and each Named Person bound by this Section is bound by Section 14 for that purpose in the way 14.4b states.

11. Non-solicitation

11.1 Restricted clients. During this Agreement and for 12 months after it ends, Contractor will not actively solicit any Restricted Client to move that client's Bridge Builders-delivered services to Contractor or to a competing provider.

A "Restricted Client" is a Bridge Builders client Contractor introduced, closed, or materially worked with. A client Contractor merely learned of is not a Restricted Client.

11.2 Contractor personnel. During this Agreement and for the period in 11.1, Contractor will not solicit another Bridge Builders contractor or Named Person of another contractor to end their engagement with Bridge Builders.

11.3 What this Section does not restrict. Contractor remains free to:

(a) sell other products and services to businesses generally, including to businesses Contractor introduced to Bridge Builders, so long as they do not compete with what Bridge Builders sells; (b) continue or expand any client relationship that existed before Contractor first introduced that client to Bridge Builders, evidenced by Contractor's records; (c) develop, market and sell its own products, provided it does not use Bridge Builders' Confidential Information to do so; (d) respond to general advertising not targeted at Restricted Clients; (e) accept an unsolicited approach from a Restricted Client, subject to 11.4.

11.4 Unsolicited approaches. If a Restricted Client approaches Contractor unsolicited during the restricted period about services of a kind Bridge Builders sells, Contractor will notify Bridge Builders in writing at least 5 business days before accepting, and will not disparage Bridge Builders or encourage the client to terminate. Contractor may then accept.

11.5 Purpose and reasonableness. This Section protects Bridge Builders' client relationships and confidential information, not Contractor's ability to earn a living. The parties agree that Bridge Builders has legitimate business interests, within the meaning of section 542.335, Florida Statutes, in its substantial relationships with the Restricted Clients, the goodwill of those clients, and its Confidential Information, and that a restriction limited to Restricted Clients and to the period in 11.1 is reasonably necessary to protect them. The engagement, and the commissions and residuals earned under it, are the consideration for this Section. If a court or arbitrator finds any part overbroad, it is to be enforced to the maximum extent permitted rather than struck.

13. Indemnity and limitation of liability

13.1 Contractor's indemnity. Contractor will defend and indemnify Bridge Builders against third-party claims, and resulting losses, arising from: (a) Contractor's breach of Sections 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 or 12; (b) Contractor's negligence or willful misconduct in performing the Services; (c) Contractor's prospecting and outreach methods, including any claim under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the CAN-SPAM Act, or any state telemarketing, do-not-call, or recording-consent law; (d) any representation Contractor made to a prospect or client that Section 10 does not permit; or (e) where Contractor is an entity, any claim by or on behalf of its Named Personnel or other personnel that they are employees of Bridge Builders, and any tax, wage or benefit claim brought by or on behalf of them, except to the extent the claim arises from Bridge Builders' own conduct outside this Agreement.

13.2 Bridge Builders' indemnity. Bridge Builders will defend and indemnify Contractor against third-party claims, and resulting losses, arising from: (a) the Bridge Builders Platform or any Bridge Builders product as designed and supplied by Bridge Builders; (b) marketing materials, claims, scripts or pricing Bridge Builders supplied or approved in writing, where Contractor used them as approved; (c) Bridge Builders' agreements with, or representations to, its clients; or (d) Bridge Builders' own breach, negligence or willful misconduct.

Contractor is not responsible for defects in the Bridge Builders Platform, or for approved materials used as approved, except to the extent Contractor knew or reasonably should have known a claim was inaccurate and used it anyway. Bridge Builders' indemnity under 13.2(b) covers the content of approved materials, not the channel, list, timing or method by which Contractor delivered them, which remain within 13.1(c).

13.3 Indemnity procedure. The indemnified party gives prompt written notice, allows the indemnifying party to control the defence with counsel of its choice, and cooperates reasonably at the indemnifying party's expense. The indemnified party may participate with its own counsel at its own cost. No settlement admitting fault by, or imposing a non-monetary obligation on, the indemnified party may be made without its written consent, not unreasonably withheld.

13.4 Limitation of liability. Except as stated in 13.5 and 13.6, each party's total aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement, whether in contract, tort (including ordinary negligence), or otherwise, is limited to the greater of (a) the total amounts paid or payable to Contractor under this Agreement in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim, or (b) $5,000.

Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, exemplary or punitive damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, lost data or business interruption. This paragraph does not limit either party's indemnity for a third-party claim under 13.1 or 13.2: amounts paid to a third party under an indemnified claim, including statutory, multiplied or exemplary damages awarded to that third party and reasonable defence costs, are direct losses for this Section and are subject only to 13.4 through 13.6a.

13.5 Prospect data super-cap. For liability arising from Contractor's failure to comply with 9.1 (no access beyond the designated handoff systems), 9.2 (no passwords, no payment), 9.3 (data stays in designated systems) or 9.5 (nothing retained after termination), the cap in 13.4 is replaced by three times the amount in 13.4(a), subject to a floor of $25,000 and a ceiling of $100,000. Any other breach of Section 9 remains subject to 13.4. This 13.5 governs that liability however the claim is framed, including as a breach of Section 7 in respect of the same data; 13.6 still applies to fraud, gross negligence and willful misconduct.

13.6 Outside every cap, and why. No cap applies to: Contractor's indemnity under 13.1(c) (prospecting methods) and 13.1(d) (unauthorised representations), so far as 13.6a provides; 13.1(e); Bridge Builders' indemnity under 13.2(a) to the extent the claim is that the Bridge Builders Platform or a Bridge Builders product infringes a third party's intellectual property; breach of Section 7 (confidentiality), other than one that 13.5 governs; infringement of the other party's intellectual property; fraud; gross negligence or willful misconduct, except for a claim within 13.1(c) or 13.1(d), which 13.6a governs; or amounts due under Section 4. Each party's remaining indemnity obligations are subject to 13.4 and 13.5.

13.6a How far 13.1(c) and 13.1(d) are uncapped. Contractor's indemnity under 13.1(c) and 13.1(d) is uncapped where the underlying conduct was knowing or willful, and otherwise capped at the greater of five times the amounts paid or payable to Contractor under this Agreement in the 12 months before the event giving rise to the claim, or $50,000. Conduct is knowing or willful for this purpose where Contractor, or the Named Person who acted: (i) knew that the conduct was unlawful or was not permitted by Section 10 or 12; (ii) continued it after Bridge Builders told Contractor in writing that it was unlawful or not permitted; (iii) contacted a recipient again after that recipient asked, in any form, not to be contacted, allowing up to 10 business days to process the request; or (iv) deliberately avoided finding out that the conduct was unlawful. It is not enough that the call, message or statement was made intentionally. This contractual standard applies regardless of the standard a court would apply under 47 U.S.C. section 227(b)(3) or any other statute. A claim within 13.1(c) or 13.1(d) is governed by this Section even if it also falls within 13.1(a) or 13.1(b), and the exclusions in 13.6 for gross negligence and willful misconduct do not apply to it separately.

13.6b Acknowledgment. Contractor acknowledges that 13.1(c) and 13.1(d) carry the largest exposure in this Agreement and that 13.6a governs it. Statutory damages under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act run per call or text and can reach five or six figures from a single campaign. That exposure is created by how Contractor chooses to prospect, a choice Bridge Builders does not make, does not direct, and cannot supervise. Section 12 states the standard Contractor must meet. Where the underlying conduct was knowing or willful under 13.6a, Contractor cannot cap the consequences at the amount in 13.4 and leave them with Bridge Builders; otherwise Contractor has the finite ceiling in 13.6a.

13.7 Insurance. Neither party undertakes to carry any particular insurance under this Agreement. Bridge Builders discloses that it does not currently carry technology errors and omissions or cyber liability insurance, and will notify Contractor in writing when it does. If Bridge Builders later requires Contractor to carry cover, it will say so in writing, specify the type and amount, and give at least 30 days to obtain it; until then none is required.

14. Dispute resolution and governing law

14.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida without regard to conflict-of-laws rules. The United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods does not apply.

14.2 Escalation. Before starting any proceeding, the parties will attempt in good faith to resolve the dispute through direct discussion for 30 days after written notice describing the dispute and the outcome sought.

14.3 Mediation. If that fails, the parties will attempt non-binding mediation for a further 30 days, by a single agreed mediator, conducted remotely, fees shared equally. If no mediator is agreed within 10 days after the 14.2 period ends, either party may ask the American Arbitration Association to appoint one; the 30 days run from the end of the 14.2 period in either case, and when they end either party may proceed under 14.4 or 14.4a. Neither party is required to travel.

14.4 Venue, where 14.4a does not apply. If mediation fails, the state courts sitting in the venue county stated in this clause and the United States district court whose district includes it have exclusive jurisdiction, and each party consents to them and waives any inconvenient-forum objection. Venue: Hernando County, Florida.

14.4a Arbitration, where Contractor is outside the United States. If mediation fails, the dispute is finally resolved by binding arbitration before a single arbitrator, seated in Hernando County, Florida, conducted in English, administered by the International Centre for Dispute Resolution of the American Arbitration Association under its International Arbitration Rules in force when the arbitration is filed. The parties agree that:

(a) the International Expedited Procedures of those Rules apply whatever the amount in dispute, and where no claim or counterclaim exceeds $100,000 (excluding interest, fees and costs) the dispute is decided on written submissions alone unless the arbitrator finds an oral hearing necessary; any hearing is held by video conference and neither party is required to travel;

(b) the arbitrator applies Florida law under 14.1, may award any remedy a Florida court could award, including injunctive relief, and may not award damages that Section 13 excludes or amounts above the caps it sets;

(c) the administrative fees and the arbitrator's compensation are shared equally, and each party bears its own attorneys' fees and costs under 14.6, except that the arbitrator may allocate fees and compensation against a party that brought or defended a claim without reasonable basis; and where Contractor is the claimant on a claim solely for unpaid amounts under Section 4 not exceeding $10,000, Bridge Builders advances the filing fee and the arbitrator's compensation and may recover them from Contractor only if the arbitrator finds the claim was brought without reasonable basis;

(d) the arbitration and the award are confidential except as needed to enforce or challenge the award; and

(e) the agreement to arbitrate is governed by the Federal Arbitration Act, the award is final and binding, judgment on it may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction, and the parties intend the award to be enforceable under the Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards.

This clause applies, and 14.4 does not, where Contractor delivers a Form W-8 under Section 15 or, before any tax form is delivered, is not a United States person under section 7701(a)(30) of the Internal Revenue Code, or where Contractor or any of its Named Personnel performs any part of the Services from outside the United States. Otherwise 14.4 applies.

14.4b Named Personnel. A Named Person who signs the joinder in Section 17 is bound by this Section 14 for any dispute about their obligations under Sections 7, 9 or 12, as if they were Contractor; 7.5, 9.7 and 12.5 carry this Section into the Sections the Named Person is a party to. 14.4a applies where the Named Person is located outside the United States, and 14.4 applies where they are not.

14.5 Exceptions. Either party may seek injunctive relief for breach of Section 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 or 12 in any court of competent jurisdiction without first completing 14.2, 14.3 or 14.4a, and seeking it is not a waiver of the arbitration agreement in 14.4a or of the arbitrator's power to grant interim relief. 14.6 Fees. Each party bears its own costs, except that a party prevailing on a claim for unpaid amounts due under Section 4 may recover reasonable costs of collection. Nothing in this Section limits a court's or arbitrator's authority under section 542.335(1)(k), Florida Statutes, to award attorney's fees and costs to the prevailing party in a proceeding to enforce or challenge Section 11.
